Serving All of Tampa Bay — From Our Home Base in Dunedin

Roof Monsters is headquartered in Dunedin, Florida and provides roofing across Pasco County, Pinellas County, Hernando County, Hillsborough County, Manatee County — the same five-county Tampa Bay territory published on roofmonsters.co.

Roof Monsters dispatches from Dunedin. Pinellas is the primary service area. We regularly take Tampa and west Pasco jobs. Extended cities (Land O' Lakes, Wesley Chapel, Brandon, Manatee, Hernando) are a fit mainly for larger replacements. We do not serve Jacksonville, Orlando, or Miami.
